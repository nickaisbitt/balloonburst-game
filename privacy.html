Balloon Burst — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

Balloon Burst ("the game", "we") is a casual tower defense game. This policy explains what data the game handles and why.

Data we collect

Game progress (best wave, medals, currency, settings) is stored locally on your device only. It is never transmitted to us and we cannot read it.

Advertising data. If you choose to allow tracking (iOS App Tracking Transparency prompt), our advertising partner (Google AdMob) may use device identifiers to show more relevant ads. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will be less relevant. See Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Data we do NOT collect

  • We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, or account registration.
  • We do not collect precise location data.
  • We do not sell personal data.

In-app purchases

Purchases (gem packs and "Remove Ads") are processed entirely by Apple's App Store. We never see or store your payment details. Purchases can be restored on any device signed into the same Apple ID via the "Restore purchases" button in the game.

Children

The game is rated for ages 4+ and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Deleting your data

All game data lives on your device. Deleting the app removes all game progress permanently. To limit ad tracking, you can decline the tracking prompt or enable "Limit Ad Tracking" in iOS Settings → Privacy → Tracking.

Changes

If we change this policy, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
