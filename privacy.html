Balloon Burst — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Balloon Burst ("the game", "we") is a casual tower defense game. This policy explains what data the game handles and why, per platform.

The web version (this site)

The web version contains no advertising, no purchases, and no analytics. Nothing you do in the game is transmitted to us or to any third party. All game progress (best wave, medals, currency, settings) lives in your browser's storage on your device only — we cannot read it, and clearing your browser data for this site erases it.

Data we collect (mobile apps)

Game progress (best wave, medals, currency, settings) is stored locally on your device only. It is never transmitted to us and we cannot read it.

Advertising data (iOS/Android apps only). If you choose to allow tracking (iOS App Tracking Transparency prompt), our advertising partner (Google AdMob) may use device identifiers to show more relevant ads. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will be less relevant. See Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Data we do NOT collect

  • We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, or account registration.
  • We do not collect precise location data.
  • We do not sell personal data.

In-app purchases (mobile apps only)

Optional purchases (gem packs and "Remove Ads") exist only in the app-store versions of the game; the web version has no purchases at all. Payments are handled entirely by the store you bought from:

  • Apple App Store (iPhone/iPad): purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We never see or store your payment details. Purchases can be restored on any device signed into the same Apple ID via the "Restore purchases" button in the game.
  • Google Play (Android): purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment details, and the same "Restore purchases" button works with your Google account.

Children

The game is rated for ages 4+ and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Deleting your data

All game data lives on your device. On the web, clearing your browser's site data for this site removes all game progress permanently. In the mobile apps, deleting the app removes all game progress permanently. To limit ad tracking (mobile apps), you can decline the tracking prompt or enable "Limit Ad Tracking" in iOS Settings → Privacy → Tracking.

Changes

If we change this policy, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
